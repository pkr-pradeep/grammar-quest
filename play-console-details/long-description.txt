Grammar Quest makes English grammar visual, clear, and genuinely fun to learn—for learners of all ages.

Guided by Śukaji, your friendly parrot mentor, along with Mira and Arjun, you explore how English sentences really work through bite-sized interactive lessons, hands-on word labs, and self-paced practice. Instead of memorising dry rules, you see grammar in action: tap any word to inspect its role, compare sentence patterns side by side, and build confidence one step at a time.

WHAT YOU CAN LEARN
Start right away with interactive lessons covering essential grammar foundations:
• Subject and Predicate – Understand how complete sentences are built and spot core subjects and predicates.
• Sentence Types & Clause Patterns – Explore sentence purposes, phrases vs. clauses, and core clause structures.
• Tenses: Time and Verb Forms – Connect past, present, and future time clearly with the right verb forms.
• Subject–Verb Agreement – Lock singular and plural subjects to matching verbs, even with tricky phrases in between.
• Active and Passive Voice – See how focus shifts between the doer and receiver of an action, step by step.
• Prepositions – Master relationships of place, time, direction, and means with visual examples.

An in-app curriculum roadmap also lets you browse upcoming grammar topics across sentences, verbs, modifiers, connectors, speech, and punctuation.

HOW GRAMMAR QUEST WORKS
• Visual Step-by-Step Lessons: Compact, mobile-friendly teaching screens with interactive starter sentences, color-coded highlights, progressive examples (Easy to Challenging), and collapsible explanations.
• Tap-to-Inspect Words: Tap any word in a sentence to immediately see its grammar role and meaning without losing your place.
• Hands-On Practice & Word Labs: Reinforce concepts with interactive sorting, sentence building, word replacement, error spotting, and independent practice activities.
• Self-Check Quizzes & Revision Tips: Test your understanding with topic quizzes that provide clear scores and focused revision guidance.
• Learn at Your Own Pace: Pause, replay animations, revisit guide notes anytime, and pick up right where you left off.

BUILT FOR FOCUSED, SAFE LEARNING
• No account or sign-up required—open the app and start learning immediately.
• Progress, practice stars, and quiz results are stored locally on your device.
• Clean, distraction-free interface with no ads and no tracking.

Whether you are brushing up on fundamentals, strengthening school or everyday writing, or helping someone build grammar confidence, Grammar Quest turns tricky rules into clear visual habits.
