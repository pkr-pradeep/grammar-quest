# Privacy Policy for Grammar Quest

**Effective Date:** November 19, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 19, 2026  

Thank you for using **Grammar Quest**. Your privacy—and the privacy of learners of all ages—matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Grammar Quest handles information when you use the app.

---

## 1. Summary

Grammar Quest is designed to be a privacy-first educational app:

- **No account required:** You do not need to create an account, sign in, or provide a name, email address, or phone number to use Grammar Quest.
- **No personal data collected:** We do not collect, sell, rent, or transmit personal information to external servers.
- **Local-only progress:** Your lesson progress, practice stars, and quiz attempts are stored strictly on your own device.
- **No ads or third-party tracking:** Grammar Quest does not display third-party advertisements and does not use third-party behavioral analytics or advertising SDKs.

---

## 2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide a smooth learning experience across visits, Grammar Quest stores non-personal learning state directly on your device using standard browser/WebView storage (`IndexedDB` and `sessionStorage`):

- **Lesson Progress (`IndexedDB`):** Which lessons and steps you have started or completed, solved practice activity IDs, and stars earned in guided practice.
- **Quiz & Assessment Attempts (`IndexedDB`):** In-progress quiz selections, submitted answers, scores, and revision suggestions so you can review your results locally.
- **Session UI State (`sessionStorage`):** Temporary session flags used only during your current visit (for example, remembering whether you have already seen a step's introductory guide note).

This locally stored data **never leaves your device** and is not transmitted to us or any third party.

---

## 3. Information We Do Not Collect

Grammar Quest does **not** collect or request access to:

- Personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number, mailing address, or user IDs)
- Precise or approximate device location
- Contacts, calendar, SMS, call logs, camera, microphone, or photo library
- Financial or payment information
- Advertising IDs or cross-app tracking identifiers

---

## 4. Network Requests & Third-Party Services

Grammar Quest runs its lessons, interactive exercises, and local progress storage directly on your device without sending your activity to our servers.

However, limited standard technical requests may occur in the following cases:

- **Typography (Google Fonts):** The app loads web fonts (`Baloo 2`, `Inter`, and `Nunito`) from Google Fonts (`fonts.googleapis.com` and `fonts.gstatic.com`) to render readable typography. When your device fetches font files, standard HTTP request metadata (such as IP address and user-agent headers) may be processed by Google in accordance with the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google Fonts FAQ](https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy).
- **App Distribution (Google Play):** If you download and install Grammar Quest from Google Play, Google may collect diagnostic, installation, or crash data according to your device settings and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We do not integrate third-party advertising networks, social login SDKs, or user-tracking analytics services.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

Grammar Quest is built for learners of all ages, including students and children. We do not knowingly collect, store, or share personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country or region). Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, parents and guardians can feel confident letting learners use the app independently.

If you are a parent or guardian and have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us using the details in Section 8.

---

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

All learning progress and quiz results remain solely on your device for as long as you keep the app installed. You have full control over this data and can permanently delete it at any time by:

1. Clearing the app's local storage in your Android device settings (**Settings → Apps → Grammar Quest → Storage & cache → Clear storage**), or
2. Uninstalling Grammar Quest from your device.

Because we do not store your data on external servers, we do not retain any copy after you clear app storage or uninstall the app.

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if we add new features or if legal or platform requirements change. Any updates will be reflected by changing the **Last Updated** date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
