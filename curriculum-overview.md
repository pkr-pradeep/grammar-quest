# Grammar Quest — Source-Based Curriculum Overview (Classes 9 & 10)

This document presents the verified, source-based English grammar curriculum extracted directly from the four supplied Indian secondary-school textbooks. No details, board attributions, edition years, or page numbers have been assumed or fabricated. Composition, vocabulary, reading comprehension, and writing sections are strictly separated from core grammar topics.

## 2. Extracted Grammar Curriculum by Textbook

### 2.1 Book 1 — Assam / SEBA: *A NEW METHOD English Grammar For Class IX & X*

#### A. Grammar Chapters (Chapters 1–17, Book pp. 1–289 / PDF pp. 10–298)
1. **Chapter 1: Sentence (वाक्य)** *(Book pp. 1–17 / PDF pp. 10–26)*
   - **L1:** Word order & complete sense (pp. 1–2).
   - **L2:** **Subject (उद्देश्य) & Predicate (विधेय)**; understood *you* in imperatives; **Finite verb** defined via agreement with Subject's Number and Person (pp. 2–3).
   - **L3:** Adjuncts (pp. 4–5).
   - **L4:** Kinds of sentences by structure; 6 kinds of Subject; 9 Subject Adjuncts (pp. 5–6).
   - **L5:** The Predicate: Transitive/Intransitive verbs, Objects (Direct, Indirect/Dative of Interest, Cognate, Reflexive, Adverbial, Retained) (pp. 6–8).
   - **L6:** Complements & Sentence Enlargement (pp. 8–9).
   - **L7–L8:** Complex and Compound Sentences (pp. 10–12).
   - **L9:** Functional sentence types (Declarative, Interrogative, Imperative, Optative, Exclamatory) + Conditionals (Probable, Improbable, Impossible, Universal fact) (pp. 12–17).
2. **Chapter 2: Phrases and Clauses** *(Book pp. 18–24 / PDF pp. 27–33)* — Phrase vs clause classification.
3. **Chapter 3: Transformation of Sentences** *(Book pp. 25–39 / PDF pp. 34–48)* — Affirmative/Negative, Affirmative/Interrogative, Question tags (lists *amn't* on p. 26), Exclamatory/Assertive.
4. **Chapter 4: Nouns** *(Book pp. 40–63 / PDF pp. 49–72)* — Countable/Uncountable, Number rules (`cattle/people/police`, plural-form singular nouns `mathematics/physics/news`, SVA drill in Ex. 4 on pp. 50–51), Gender, Case.
5. **Chapter 5: Pronouns** *(Book pp. 64–73 / PDF pp. 73–82)* — 9 pronoun classes; SVA rules for `and`, `each/every`, `either...or/neither...nor`, `each of/neither of` (pp. 66–67) & Error Exercise 2 (p. 73).
6. **Chapter 6: Determinatives** *(Book pp. 74–101 / PDF pp. 83–110)* — Articles (Rule 14 p. 80: *"The Secretary and Headmaster is coming"* vs *"The Secretary and the Headmaster are coming"*), Adjectives (p. 84: `Many a man` + singular vs `A great many men` + plural), Phrases, Apposition.
7. **Chapter 7: Clauses** *(Book pp. 102–112 / PDF pp. 111–121)* — Subordinate Noun, Adjective, and Adverb clauses.
8. **Chapter 8: Analysis (Clause Analysis)** *(Book pp. 113–119 / PDF pp. 122–128)* — Principal and subordinate clause parsing.
9. **Chapter 9: Tense (काल)** *(Book pp. 120–147 / PDF pp. 129–156)*
   - **L1:** Time vs Tense (pp. 120–121).
   - **L2–L3:** **Explicit 3-Tense model** (Present, Past, Future) × 4 Aspects = **12 Tenses** using dual labels (*Indefinite/Simple, Continuous/Progressive, Perfect/Non-Progressive, Perfect Continuous/Perfect Progressive*, pp. 121–126).
   - **L4:** Strong, Weak, Invariant, and Mixed Verbs (pp. 126–133).
   - **L5:** Structural conjugation tables across all 12 tenses and persons/numbers (pp. 134–137).
   - **L6:** Tense uses, non-continuous stative verbs, `for/since`, 15 Common Errors, and translation/correction exercises (pp. 137–147).
10. **Chapter 10: Special Verbs** *(Book pp. 148–185 / PDF pp. 157–194)* — Finite vs Non-finite & **24 Anomalous Finites** (pp. 148–159), Non-conclusive verbs (pp. 159–161), Inchoative verbs (pp. 161–165), Group/Phrasal Verbs (pp. 166–185).
11. **Chapter 11: Voice** *(Book pp. 186–193 / PDF pp. 195–202)*.
12. **Chapter 12: Conjunctives and Relatives** *(Book pp. 194–204 / PDF pp. 203–213; Ex. A item 10 p. 201 tests "Bread and butter is/are")*.
13. **Chapter 13: Adverbials** *(Book pp. 205–213 / PDF pp. 214–222)*.
14. **Chapter 14: Position of Prepositions** *(Book pp. 214–241 / PDF pp. 223–250)*.
15. **Chapter 15: Reported Speech** *(Book pp. 242–259 / PDF pp. 251–268)*.
16. **Chapter 16: Transformation** *(Book pp. 260–281 / PDF pp. 269–290)* — Simple/Complex/Compound & Degrees of Comparison.
17. **Chapter 17: Punctuation** *(Book pp. 282–289 / PDF pp. 291–298)*.

#### B. Excluded Non-Grammar Sections (Vocabulary / Word Knowledge, Ch. 18–23, Book pp. 290–344 / PDF pp. 299–353)
- Synonyms (Ch. 18), Antonyms (Ch. 19), Similarly Pronounced & Compound Words (Ch. 20), Phrases & Idioms (Ch. 21), Single-Word Substitution (Ch. 22), Diminutives (Ch. 23).

### 2.2 Book 2 — Punjab / PSEB: *A Practice Book of ENGLISH GRAMMAR & COMPOSITION (GRAMMAR IN USE) for Class IX*

#### A. Grammar Chapters (Chapters 1–9, Book pp. 1–104 / PDF pp. 7–110)
1. **Chapter 1: Determiners** *(Book pp. 1–11 / PDF pp. 7–17)* — Articles, Demonstratives, Possessives, Numerals, Quantifiers.
2. **Chapter 2: Modals** *(Book pp. 12–26 / PDF pp. 18–32)* — Usage & practice exercises.
3. **Chapter 3: Prepositions** *(Book pp. 27–40 / PDF pp. 33–46)* — Time, place, direction, fixed prepositional uses.
4. **Chapter 4: Conjunctions** *(Book pp. 41–52 / PDF pp. 47–58)* — Co-ordinating, subordinating, correlative conjunctions; `If` conditionals table (p. 43).
5. **Chapter 5: Simple and Complex Sentences** *(Book pp. 53–60 / PDF pp. 59–66)* — Briefly notes *"A simple sentence has one subject and one predicate"* with two underlined examples (p. 53) before covering Noun, Adjective, and Adverb clauses.
6. **Chapter 6: Voice** *(Book pp. 61–73 / PDF pp. 67–79)* — Active/Passive rules across tenses and modals.
7. **Chapter 7: Narration** *(Book pp. 74–87 / PDF pp. 80–93)* — Direct/Indirect speech rules.
8. **Chapter 8: Non-Finites** *(Book pp. 88–93 / PDF pp. 94–99)* — Infinitives (`to+V1`, bare), Gerunds, Participles.
9. **Chapter 9: Tenses (Concord and Sequence)** *(Book pp. 94–104 / PDF pp. 100–110)*
   - **Section 1: Use of Tenses** *(pp. 94–97)* — **12 traditional tenses** (Present, Past, Future × Indefinite, Continuous, Perfect, Perfect Continuous); assumes conjugation tables are already known.
   - **Section 2: Concord (Agreement of the Verb with Subject)** *(pp. 97–100)* — **13 numbered rules + Exceptions**:
     - Number & Person / Head-noun agreement (`The quality of these apples is good`); `and` plural vs single idea (`Slow and steady wins`) / same person / `each & every`; `or / either...or / neither...nor` (singular, plural last, nearest person); `with` / `as well as`; `not only...but also`; `there`; `either/neither/each/everyone/one of` + singular; plural-form singular nouns (`Mathematics, Politics, wages, news, United States, Gulliver's Travels`); collective nouns; relative pronoun antecedents; sums of money.
   - **Section 3: Sequence of Tenses** *(pp. 100–102)* + **Exercises 1–6** *(pp. 102–104: Ex. 1 tenses; Ex. 2–6 Concord)*.

#### B. Excluded Non-Grammar Composition Section (Book pp. 105–162 / PDF pp. 111–168)
- Ch. 1 Vocabulary (pp. 105–129); Ch. 2 Reading Skills — Prose & Poems (pp. 130–136); Ch. 3 Writing Skills-I — Note-making, E-mail, Letters (pp. 137–157); Ch. 4 Writing Skills-II — Paragraph Writing (pp. 158–162).

### 2.3 Book 3 — Odisha BSE: *LEARN AND PRACTISE GRAMMAR CLASS - IX*

#### Grammar Chapters (All 12 Chapters are Grammar, Book pp. 1–146 / PDF pp. 7–152; no composition section)
1. **Chapter 1: Parts of a Sentence** *(Book pp. 1–26 / PDF pp. 7–32)*
   - **A. Word Order** (pp. 1–4); **B. Subject and Predicate** — single vs multi-word, **Head-word (noun vs verb)**, split predicates in questions, understood *you* in imperatives, omitted *it/is* in exclamations (pp. 5–7, Activities 1–9); **C. Word Classes** (8 parts of speech including 5 semantic verb classes, pp. 8–22); **D. Types of Sentences** (4 functional types of simple sentence, pp. 23–26).
2. **Chapter 2: Verbs** *(Book pp. 27–32 / PDF pp. 33–38)* — Main vs Auxiliary (Primary `be/have/do` & Modal), multi-word verb groups (`aux + mv-en/-ing`), **Introductory SVA table on p. 32** (*"You will learn more about subject-verb agreement later"*).
3. **Chapter 3: Time and Tense** *(Book pp. 33–61 / PDF pp. 39–67)* — Time (straight line: Past/Present/Future) vs Tense (**explicitly teaches ONLY TWO TENSES: Present and Past; "there is no future tense in English"**); 3 verb forms (`v`, `v-ed`, `-ed/-en`); 8 Tense × Aspect cells; Stative verbs (p. 43); `for/since`, `used to/would`, and **Future Time expressions** (modals, `be going to + v`, Present Progressive, Present Simple, `be about to`, `will be v-ing`, `will have v-en`, `be + to + infinitive`, pp. 57–61).
4. **Chapter 4: Auxiliaries** *(Book pp. 62–73 / PDF pp. 68–79)* — Primary vs Modal (Paired + Pure/Semi), 6 properties of modals.
5. **Chapter 5: The Noun Phrase** *(Book pp. 74–77 / PDF pp. 80–83)* — Constituents & tree structures.
6. **Chapter 6: Pre and Post Modifiers in the Noun Phrase** *(Book pp. 78–81 / PDF pp. 84–87)*.
7. **Chapter 7: Countables and Uncountables** *(Book pp. 82–91 / PDF pp. 88–97)* — SVA with count/uncount nouns (p. 83), pair nouns vs `a pair of` (p. 85 Act. 4), collective nouns & `police/people/cattle` (p. 86), singular `-s` uncountables (`Mathematics/Diabetes/Gymnastics`, pp. 88–89), quantifying units (p. 90).
8. **Chapter 8: Determiners** *(Book pp. 92–103 / PDF pp. 98–109)* — Includes quantifier SVA rules (p. 93) & `The rich/blind` + plural verb (pp. 98–99).
9. **Chapter 9: Adjectives** *(Book pp. 104–116 / PDF pp. 110–122)* — Positions, ordering, Degrees of Comparison.
10. **Chapter 10: Adverbs and Adverbials** *(Book pp. 117–134 / PDF pp. 123–140)* — Forms, positions, PMT/MPT order, Negative inversion (`Rarely/Never/Seldom`).
11. **Chapter 11: Negatives and Interrogatives** *(Book pp. 135–140 / PDF pp. 141–146)* — `do`-support, contractions (`I'm not`, NOT `I amn't`, p. 136), Yes/No & Wh-questions.
12. **Chapter 12: The Predicate Phrase** *(Book pp. 141–146 / PDF pp. 147–152)* — Transitive/Intransitive clause patterns (`S+V`, `S+V+C`, `S+V+O`, `S+V+O+O`, `S+V+O+C`, `S+V+A`), Direct/Indirect objects, Adjuncts vs Complements.

### 2.4 Book 4 — Odisha BSE: *Learn and Practise Grammar Class - X*

#### Grammar Chapters (All 10 Chapters are Grammar, Book pp. 1–122 / PDF pp. 7–128; no composition section)
1. **Chapter 1: Tense Revisited** *(Book pp. 1–12 / PDF pp. 7–18)* — Reaffirms **2 defined tenses (Present & Past)** expressing **3 phases of time**; Present Simple/Progressive; 6 ways of expressing Past Time; 5 ways of expressing Future Time.
2. **Chapter 2: Types of Sentences** *(Book pp. 13–22 / PDF pp. 19–28)* — Finite verb & clause definition; Simple sentence types & patterns (`SV, SVO, SVOO, SVC, SVOC`, pp. 14–15); Compound & Complex sentences.
3. **Chapter 3: Subject – Verb Agreement** *(Book pp. 23–30 / PDF pp. 29–36)* — Core Number & Person rule + **16 numbered rules (`1a`–`16`)**: `there`/`it`; plural sums/measurements; `and` single notion vs compound; collectives & `police/people/poultry/cattle`; uncountables; intervening clauses; `either/neither/none of` (+ informal plural note); `each/one of`; `no/some`; `and` with 1 vs 2 determiners; fractions; `a lot of / crowd of / a number of vs the number of`; `-s` singular nouns (`Statistics/Physics/News/Measles`); clause subjects; `with/as well as`; `not only...but also / either...or / neither...nor` (nearest subject); pair nouns; `The rich/blind`; plural proper names; relative pronoun antecedents + Activities 1–3.
4. **Chapter 4: Non-Finite Verb Forms** *(Book pp. 31–38 / PDF pp. 37–44)* — Links **finite verb = subject agreement** vs non-finite = no agreement (p. 31); covers `to+v`, bare `φ+v`, `-en`, `-ing`.
5. **Chapter 5: Prepositions** *(Book pp. 39–57 / PDF pp. 45–63)*.
6. **Chapter 6: Phrasal Verbs** *(Book pp. 58–70 / PDF pp. 64–76)*.
7. **Chapter 7: Noun Clauses and Relative Clauses** *(Book pp. 71–81 / PDF pp. 77–87)* — Defining vs non-defining relative clauses.
8. **Chapter 8: Adverb Clauses** *(Book pp. 82–89 / PDF pp. 88–95)* — Includes Zero/First/Second/Third Conditionals (pp. 84–86) & Sentence Types review (pp. 86–89).
9. **Chapter 9: The Passive** *(Book pp. 90–105 / PDF pp. 96–111)* — Uses `be + en` notation.
10. **Chapter 10: Direct and Indirect Speech** *(Book pp. 106–122 / PDF pp. 112–128)*.

## 4. Class 9 vs Class 10 Curriculum Progression Across Boards

| Board / Series | Class 9 Focus | Class 10 Focus | Curriculum Boundary Assessment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
